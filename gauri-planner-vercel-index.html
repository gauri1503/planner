Negative marking ka simple niyam

Galat par 1/3 number katta hai. Agar 4 options hain aur andaza bilkul andhera hai, to expected fayda 0 hai. Agar ek option bhi pakka kaat sako, to attempt karna fayde ka hai. Do option kat jayein to zaroor attempt karo. Paper mein pehle wo sawal karo jo aate hain, baad mein andaze wale.

Syllabus aur PYQ readiness

Overall readiness: 0%

UPSC ke official scheme ke 9 subjects. Exam 2 ghante ka objective paper hai (Hindi aur English), galat jawab par 1/3 negative marking. Har subject mein topics tick karo, phir PYQ solve karke solved, sahi aur galat bharo. Readiness = aadha syllabus coverage + aadha PYQ score (negative marking ke baad). Topics ka breakdown sirf ek suggestion hai.
